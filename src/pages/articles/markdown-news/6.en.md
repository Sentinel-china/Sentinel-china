2025 has come to a complete close. Throughout this year, we have continued to focus on the IO-Link system ecosystem, launching multiple product series from temperature measurement and I/O access to master station connection, signal conversion, and end-point execution.

Looking back at the full year, we hope to provide a clear review of these new products through an article, offering you a complete reference guide for the annual new product catalog.

## March: IO-Link Compact Temperature Transmitter
![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783211.png)
**March 7th** released the IO-Link compact temperature transmitter **THH06E-G1/4-Lxx-IL-SC**. It belongs to the process temperature measurement category, targeting common temperature point collection and system access on site. The product features compact installation, with a probe made of **316L stainless steel** material; the signal side provides both **IO-Link communication** and **4–20 mA analog output**, making it more suitable for scenarios requiring both digital access and compatibility with traditional analog systems as a temperature entry point.

## April: Adaptive I/O and Modbus/TCP IO-Link Master Station
![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783212.png)
**April 11th** released the adaptive input/output bus I/O module **ELPN-16SAP-L001**. It belongs to the discrete I/O module category, providing **16 PNP input/output adaptive channels**, used for common switch signal point collection and actuator control applications. The positioning of this product is to make I/O port capabilities more flexible "channel resources", facilitating faster adaptation during equipment point planning and debugging stages.

![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783213.png)
**April 25th** released the Modbus/TCP protocol IO-Link master station **ELMT-8IOL-L001** and **ELMT-8IOL-L04B**. Both products are Ethernet-type IO-Link master stations: **L001 has 8 Class A ports**, **L04B has 4A+4B mixed ports**. The interface adopts **dual M12 D-code Ethernet interfaces** with built-in switches, address configuration supports **DHCP and static IP**, suitable for IO-Link device access and management in Modbus/TCP network environments (sensors, actuators, IO-Link sub-stations, etc.).

![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783214.png)
**April 30th** released the input/output adaptive IO-Link sub-station and input/output adaptive expandable IO-Link sub-station. They belong to the "IO-Link end aggregation" category: the base sub-station provides **16-channel digital I/O** capabilities; the expandable sub-station adds module-level expansion and cascading on this basis, targeting multi-point, applications requiring expansion paths, providing more consistent product choices for end I/O aggregation and subsequent expansion.

## May-June: EtherCAT IO-Link Master Station and Thermocouple IO-Link Sub-station
![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783215.png)
**May 30th** released the EtherCAT protocol **L-coded IO-Link master station**. This product is positioned for IO-Link access in EtherCAT fieldbus environments, introducing **M12 L-coded high-current power interfaces** on the hardware side, providing **8 ports** and **Class A / 4A+4B** port combination options, equipped with **dual M12 D-code Ethernet interfaces** and built-in switching capabilities, suitable for production lines with high requirements for real-time performance, device density, and power supply.

![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783216.png)
**June 27th** released the thermocouple input IO-Link sub-station **SIOL-M12-8TC series**, covering **K-type, C-type and J-type thermocouples**. This series belongs to the temperature collection extension category, with the core feature of **8-channel thermocouple input**, using a unified **M12 five-pin interface**, compatible with **IO-Link V1.1**. In systems requiring multi-zone temperature measurement and centralized collection and unified access in high-temperature equipment, it acts as a standardized interface that directly aggregates "thermocouple points" into IO-Link channels.

## August: Full Series IO-Link Converters and Food-grade Flush Membrane Pressure Sensors
![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783217.png)
**August 1st** released the full series **IO-Link converters**. This series belongs to the "single-point signal access conversion" category, targeting common legacy signal types on site, covering applications such as **current input/output, voltage input/output, RTD temperature input**, and unified **IO-Link V1.1** and **M12 A-code interface**. It is more suitable for "only needing to access a small number of analog/temperature signal points" retrofit or new addition scenarios, serving as a single-point, lean IO-Link access port choice.

![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783218.png)
**August 21st** released the food-grade flush membrane pressure sensor. This series belongs to the hygienic process pressure measurement product category, with a pressure range covering **-1～600 bar**, accuracy **±0.5% F.S**, supporting **IO-Link communication**, and compatible with switch and analog outputs. Structurally adopting **flush membrane design**, the membrane material is **titanium alloy corrugated membrane**, more suitable for process applications requiring frequent cleaning and high hygiene standards.

## October: IO-Link Relay Module
![](http://image.sentinel-china.com/783219.png)
**October 17th** released the IO-Link relay module system, composed of the main module **SIOL-TL-16RM1S** and extension modules **REX-8R1S** / **REX-16R1S**, used to make relay outputs into expandable modular action layer capabilities.

Among them, **SIOL-TL-16RM1S** is a 16-channel relay module, IO-Link side compatible with **IO-Link V1.1**, adopting **Class A interface**, communication rate **COM2 (38.4 kbit/s)**, supporting **ISDU** and **data storage (DS)**; the process data side provides IO-Link output bytes and supports extension bit usage. Relay contact capacity is **5A 250VAC / 5A 30VDC**, with extension ports for connecting subsequent extension modules.

Extension modules **REX-8R1S** and **REX-16R1S** provide 8-channel and 16-channel relay extension capabilities respectively, with contact capacity also **5A 250VAC / 5A 30VDC**, used as extension sub-modules of the expandable relay module system.

## Conclusion
In 2025, we released six major categories of new products around the IO-Link system link:

- **Temperature transmitters** — Compact process temperature entry points
- **Adaptive I/O and sub-stations** — Flexible point collection and expansion capabilities
- **Modbus/TCP and EtherCAT IO-Link master stations** — Multi-protocol fieldbus access
- **Thermocouple collection sub-stations** — Multi-channel high-temperature measurement aggregation
- **IO-Link converter full series** — Single-point access for legacy signals
- **Relay module system** — Expandable end execution layer

These products constitute a complete product system from "sensing end→transmission layer→execution end", covering common industrial scenarios such as temperature, pressure, switch signals, and analog signals. We hope that by continuously enriching the IO-Link ecosystem product line, field system access can be more standardized, expansion more flexible, and operation and maintenance simpler.
